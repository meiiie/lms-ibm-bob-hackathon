# Prepare a truthful submission

Read `docs/SUBMISSION-CHECKLIST.md` and inspect actual artifacts. Check repo/demo
links, reproducible setup, video duration/format, slides and cover requirements.
Verify that `bob_sessions/` has real task summary screenshots covering relevant
work by every participant, with a useful manifest. Empty templates do not qualify.

Read the measurement records and distinguish results from estimates. Build the
narrative around one developer problem, the working flow, Bob's specific role,
verified impact, limitations and the next step. Keep all formats consistent.

Review data-source records and public artifacts for accidental credentials or
private information. Do not fabricate images, metrics, sessions or URLs; report
missing items and the shortest way to obtain them. Do not present a screenshot of
the browser tooling check as hackathon product evidence or Bob usage evidence.

Update the submission copy and demo script. Publishing, uploading or final form
submission requires the user's instruction for that action; preparing local
files does not submit them. Deadline is 22:00 Vietnam on 27 September 2026;
target submission by 20:00. Do not assume an automatic late-submission extension.
